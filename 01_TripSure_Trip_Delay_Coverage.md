# TripSure Travel Insurance

## Trip Delay Coverage Policy

**Document owner:** Claims and Policy Operations  
**Applies to:** TripSure travel protection plans that list Trip Delay as an included benefit  
**Knowledge-base status:** Approved for customer support and claim-intake grounding  

This document summarizes TripSure's Trip Delay benefit. The customer's policy schedule and certificate of insurance control if they differ from this summary.

---

## TS-TRIP-4.1 — Eligibility and Covered Delay Events

Trip Delay coverage may apply when an insured traveler is delayed for **six consecutive hours or more** after the scheduled departure time because of a covered event.

Covered events are limited to:

- severe weather that prevents the scheduled common carrier from operating;
- mechanical failure of the scheduled common carrier;
- an unannounced strike affecting the scheduled common carrier;
- a traffic accident involving the insured traveler while traveling directly to the departure point, when documented by a police or official incident report; or
- loss or theft of the insured traveler's passport or required travel documents, when documented by the appropriate authority.

Coverage applies only when the policy was active before the covered event became known or reasonably foreseeable. A delay caused solely by a missed alarm, personal scheduling error, insufficient connection time, or undocumented change of plans is not covered.

**Grounding note:** Do not describe an event as covered unless it matches one of the listed events and the minimum delay duration is satisfied. If either fact is unknown, request clarification or route the case for human review.

---

## TS-TRIP-4.2 — Covered Expenses and Benefit Limits

For an eligible Trip Delay, TripSure may reimburse reasonable, necessary, and unused expenses incurred during the delay for:

- meals;
- local transportation;
- lodging; and
- nonrefundable prepaid travel arrangements missed because of the covered delay.

Reimbursement is based on actual documented expenses and is limited to **USD 250 per insured traveler per day**, up to a **maximum Trip Delay benefit of USD 1,000 per insured traveler per covered trip**.

TripSure does not reimburse expenses paid or reimbursed by an airline, hotel, tour operator, credit-card benefit, employer, or another insurer. Luxury upgrades, alcohol, entertainment, fines, and purchases unrelated to the delay are not covered.

Benefit amounts are not guaranteed during intake. Final eligibility and payment are determined after policy validation, document review, duplicate-payment checks, and application of the customer's policy terms.

**Grounding note:** Never estimate or promise a payment. State the applicable limits and explain that the approved amount depends on documented loss and claim review.

---

## TS-TRIP-4.3 — Exclusions, Customer Duties, and Escalation

Trip Delay coverage does not apply to a delay caused by:

- a known or foreseeable event existing before the policy effective date;
- the traveler's decision not to travel when the scheduled common carrier remained available;
- unlawful acts by the insured traveler;
- intoxication or non-prescribed controlled-substance use by the insured traveler;
- routine carrier schedule changes announced before the trip; or
- circumstances otherwise excluded by the customer's policy.

The traveler must take reasonable steps to limit additional loss, retain itemized receipts, seek available reimbursement from the travel supplier, and notify TripSure promptly.

Route the matter to a licensed claims specialist when policy status is unclear, facts conflict, an exclusion may apply, fraud indicators are present, another payer may be responsible, or the customer disputes an intake explanation. The AI assistant may collect information and explain this policy summary, but it may not approve, deny, or settle a claim.

**Approved customer wording:** “I can help collect your claim details and explain the relevant TripSure policy information. A claims specialist will review your policy, documents, and circumstances before making a coverage decision.”
